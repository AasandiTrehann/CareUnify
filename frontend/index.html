Platform Core Integration Pillars

AI-powered microservices working in harmony to compile the Golden Record.

cloud_upload

Multi-Modal Ingest

Ingest data from REST APIs, HL7 feeds, lab CSV tables, scanned clinical PDFs (Tesseract OCR), and audio voice notes (Whisper dictations).

fingerprint

Entity Resolution

Phonetic blocking keys (Soundex/NYSIIS) combined with weighted string similarity and ML classifiers to score records.

psychology

RAG Intelligence

Conversational natural language search over patient histories powered by local Llama 3 models, citing references from source documents.
